# Dashboard Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots of the Power BI dashboard developed for the NHS Patient Flow & Waiting Time Analytics project.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level operational summary including:

- Total Patients
- Total Admissions
- Total Non-Admissions
- Average Waiting Time
- Average Satisfaction Score
- Patient Volume by Department
- Waiting Time by Department
- Satisfaction Score by Department

![Executive Overview](Executive_Overview.png)

---

### 2. Patient Experience & Equity Analysis

Focuses on healthcare equity and patient outcomes across demographic groups.

Key metrics include:

- Waiting Time by Ethnicity
- Satisfaction Score by Ethnicity
- Admission Outcomes by Gender
- Admission Outcomes by Ethnicity

![Patient Experience & Equity Analysis](Patient_Experience_Equity_Analysis.png)

---

### 3. Patient Demographics & Segmentation

Provides demographic insights into the patient population.

Key metrics include:

- Age Distribution
- Gender Distribution
- Ethnicity Distribution
- Waiting Time by Age Group

![Patient Demographics & Segmentation](Patient_Demographics_Segmentation.png)
